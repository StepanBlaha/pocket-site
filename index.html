Pocket

Your handheld games, saved and safe. For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Pocket plays the handheld games you own, with saves you don't have to think about: your exact spot, extra save slots and a second run for every game, touch controls you can rearrange, fast-forward, automatic ROM-hack patching and your iPhone as a controller for the Mac.

No games are included. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Pocket for Mac plays GameCube, Wii and 3DS too.
