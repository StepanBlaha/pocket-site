Sixty hours in. Still there.

Lost saves are the number one complaint about emulators: a save state overwriting the real save, or a tap on the wrong button. Pocket is built so that can't happen.

Three buttons. No traps.

  • Save keeps your exact spot. Exit never overwrites anything.
  • Next time you continue from whichever is newer: your Save or the game's own save.
  • Runs: start the game over without touching your main playthrough.
  • Your in-game save is backed up every 10 minutes. Go back to any backup in two taps.

Questions

Is Pocket free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account and nothing locked behind a payment.

Are games included?

No. Pocket plays the games you own. Add your own game files from Files, AirDrop or iCloud Drive with the + button.

Which systems does it play?

Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance and Nintendo DS on iPhone and iPad. Pocket for Mac also plays GameCube, Wii and 3DS.

Can I lose my save?

Pocket is built so you don't. Exit never overwrites your progress, the newer of your Save and your in-game save always wins, and your in-game save is backed up every 10 minutes so you can go back to an older one.

Can I bring saves from another emulator?

Yes. Pocket imports and exports plain .sav files, and backs up your current save before replacing it.
