Pocket Support

Help for Pocket on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Contact

Email stepa15.b@gmail.com. Pocket is made by one independent developer, Stepan Blaha (Czech Republic), and every message is read. Replies usually come within a few days.

Adding games

Pocket does not include any games. Tap + in the library and pick game files you own from Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop, or copy them into the Pocket folder in the Files app (On My iPhone > Pocket). Free homebrew games from independent developers work too.

Saves

In-game saves are kept automatically. Open the menu while playing and tap Save to keep your exact spot; Exit without saving continues from your last Save or in-game save, whichever is newer. Your saves stay on your device; you can export or import a save from a game's menu in the library.

ROM hacks

Add an IPS, UPS or BPS patch next to its game and Pocket builds the hack for you.

Controllers

Bluetooth game controllers work as soon as they are connected. With Pocket for Mac, your iPhone can also be the controller: open Phone Controller on the Mac, then in the iPhone app open the ⋯ menu > Use as Controller and scan the code. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Something not working?

Email the name of the game, your device and what happened. A screenshot helps.
