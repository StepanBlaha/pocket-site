Pocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Pocket is a game player for iPhone, iPad and Mac made by one independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Pocket does not collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Pocket has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no tracking. The developer never receives your games, your saves, your device details or anything you do in the app.

What stays on your device

Your game files, save files, save backups, saved spots, runs, covers you pick and your settings are stored only on your device, inside Pocket's own folder. You can see and remove them in the Files app (On My iPhone or On My iPad > Pocket) or by deleting the app.

iCloud Drive (optional)

If you turn on the iCloud Drive copy, Pocket writes copies of your in-game saves to the Pocket folder in your own iCloud Drive so your other devices can use them. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it. Turn the option off at any time; to remove the copies, delete the folder in iCloud Drive.

Cover images (optional)

When you choose box-art covers or browse covers for a game, Pocket asks GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com and api.github.com, which host the open libretro-thumbnails image collection) for an image that matches the game's name. The request contains only the game's title and the system it runs on. Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address when your device makes the request; see GitHub's privacy statement at https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Images are saved on your device so they are not fetched again. If you keep the pixel-art covers or choose your own picture from Photos, no request is made.

For some ROM hacks, the cover image comes from the LaunchBox Games Database (images.launchbox-app.com). The request contains only the hack's name, and the same applies as above: LaunchBox can see your IP address when your device makes the request.

RetroAchievements (optional)

If you sign in to RetroAchievements, Pocket talks to retroachievements.org, a separate service with its own privacy policy (https://retroachievements.org/privacy). Your username and password are sent once to get a sign-in key; the password is not stored, and the key is kept in your device's keychain. While you play a game, Pocket sends RetroAchievements a fingerprint (hash) of the game file so it can find that game's achievements, and tells it which achievements you unlock. Nothing is sent if you don't sign in. Signing out removes the key.

Camera (optional)

Pocket uses the camera only to scan the QR code that Pocket on your Mac shows, so your iPhone can act as a controller. The camera picture is processed on your device, is not saved and is not sent anywhere.

Local network (optional)

When you use your iPhone as a controller for Pocket on your Mac, the two devices talk directly over your local Wi-Fi network. Only button presses and motion (tilt and movement of the phone) are sent, and only to your Mac. Nothing goes over the internet.

Photos (optional)

If you pick a cover from Photos, you choose a single image with the system picker. Pocket copies only that image and does not get access to the rest of your library.

Children

Pocket does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
